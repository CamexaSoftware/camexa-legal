# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 20 November 2026**

Welcome to Camexa Coditor, developed by Camexa Software.

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app accesses, how it is handled, and how your privacy is protected when you use Camexa or Camexa Pro.

Our goal is simple: your files and personal information stay on your device.

## 1. About the App

Camexa Coditor is a code and text editor for Android phones. It allows users to open, edit, save and share code and text files stored on their devices.

The app is available in two versions:

- **Camexa:** the free version.
- **Camexa Pro:** the paid version with additional features.

Both versions are developed by Camexa Software.

## 2. Information We Collect

We do not collect, store or send your personal information to our servers.

We do not operate our own servers, and the app does not require you to create an account or log in.

We do not use analytics or tracking tools to monitor your activities.

Your files, code and edits remain on your device.

If you have allowed it in your Android settings, Google Play may provide us with anonymous crash reports. We do not receive your files or personal details in them.

## 3. Information Stored on Your Device

Camexa stores only a small amount of information locally on your device to provide a better experience. This includes:

- **App settings:** your selected theme, font size, word wrap and other preferences.
- **Recently opened files:** a list of recently opened file paths, which helps the app restore your previous session.

This information stays on your device and is never sent to Camexa Software.

## 4. Files and Storage Permission

The app requests access to files and storage on your device. This permission is required so you can:

- Open your own files.
- Edit existing files.
- Save changes to files.
- Work with code and text documents stored on your device.

This permission is used only to provide these features. We do not use it to collect or upload your files to our servers.

## 5. Sharing Files

When you choose to share a file or text, it is sent to the app you select. That app's own privacy policy applies to what it does with the file.

## 6. Payments and Subscriptions

Camexa Pro is available as a subscription through Google Play.

All payments and subscription transactions are handled by Google Play. We do not see or store your payment card details, banking information or other payment credentials.

Google Play tells the app whether your subscription is active, so the app can unlock Pro features. Google handles payment information according to its own privacy policy and terms.

## 7. Advertising

Camexa currently does not display advertisements.

We may introduce advertisements in the future. If we do, we will update this Privacy Policy first, to explain how advertising works and to name the advertising provider.

## 8. Children's Privacy

Camexa is not designed for children under the age of 18.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## 9. Data Security

Since your files and edits remain on your device, we do not receive or store them on our servers.

You are responsible for keeping your important files safe. We recommend keeping backup copies of your important work.

## 10. Deleting Your Information

You can remove the information stored by the app at any time by:

- Clearing Camexa's app data in your Android device settings.
- Uninstalling the app from your device.

These actions remove the app's stored settings and recent file information. They do not delete your original documents or files saved elsewhere on your device.

Uninstalling the app does not cancel an active Google Play subscription. You can cancel it in Google Play under Payments and subscriptions > Subscriptions.

## 11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app's features, services or privacy practices change. When we make important changes, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date.
